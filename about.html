About Community Helping Hands

Community Helping Hands is a local organisation focused on supporting people in need.


We believe that small acts of kindness can make a big difference. Our programmes focus on food donations, clothing drives, education and community support.


Our Mission

Our mission is to bring people together and provide support to individuals and families within our community.


Our Vision

Our vision is to create a stronger community where everyone has an opportunity to live a better life.
